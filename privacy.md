# FitBoi Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 September 2026_

FitBoi is a workout planner made by Steffen Opheim. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

## What FitBoi stores
Your profile (goal, experience, equipment, bodyweight), your workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort ratings), daily check-ins (sleep, soreness), bodyweight entries and achievements.

## Where it is stored
On your device, and in **your own private iCloud database** (CloudKit) if you are signed in to iCloud. We do not run servers that store your data and we cannot access your iCloud data. Deleting the app's data in Settings → Data → "Erase all my data" removes it from the device and from iCloud.

## AI coaching (FitBoi Pro)
When you subscribe to Pro, the app sends a summary of your training data — recovery estimates, recent sessions, today's plan, effort ratings and check-ins — to Anthropic's Claude API through a relay we operate, in order to generate your session brief and debrief. This data is used only to produce that response and is subject to [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy). No name, email or account identifier is sent; requests carry an anonymous per-device identifier used solely for rate limiting.

## What we do not do
No accounts, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

## Imports
CSV files you import from other apps are read on the device only.
